\# Product Requirements Document: Mellow



\## Problem

Remote workers and students often experience high stress and lack a quick, frictionless way to ground themselves before starting deep work. Existing meditation apps are too complex, requiring accounts, long time commitments, and visual clutter.



\## Solution

"Mellow" is a zero-friction, single-purpose web app that guides users through a 4-4-4 box breathing exercise. It uses Neumorphism (Soft UI) design to create a calming, tactile, and visually soothing experience that feels like a physical object on the screen.



\## Target Audience

Knowledge workers, students, and developers who need a 2-minute mental reset without leaving their browser or creating an account.



\## Success Metrics

\- The app loads instantly with zero configuration.

\- The user can complete one full breathing cycle without confusion.

\- The UI successfully conveys a "soft, calming" aesthetic (validated by design criteria).
